Nibbles Support

Need help? Email us at hello@thenibblesapp.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Nibbles. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

Already subscribed on another device? Open the subscription screen in Nibbles and tap Restore purchase.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and your baby's data from inside the app: open your Profile and choose Delete account. To ask us to delete your data instead, email hello@thenibblesapp.com from the address on your account. Full steps are on our account deletion page.

Privacy requests

To access, correct or delete your information, or to make a privacy complaint, email us with "Privacy Request" or "Privacy Complaint" in the subject line. See our Privacy Policy for details.
